## 辨析要点

**heritage vs tradition vs custom**
- heritage = 遗产（物质/非物质，可传承）
- tradition = 传统（长期形成的做法）
- custom = 习俗（特定群体的习惯做法）

**artistic vs creative**
- artistic = 有艺术天赋/审美能力（偏天赋）
- creative = 有创造力（偏能力）

**classical vs contemporary**
- classical = 古典的（过去的经典）
- contemporary = 当代的（现在的）

## 高考真题语境练习

1. The temple is part of our national ______ and must be protected. (heritage)
2. It's a local ______ to eat dumplings during Spring Festival. (custom)
3. The ______ of the building reflects both modern and traditional styles. (architecture)
4. The ______ cheered loudly when the singer appeared on stage. (audience)
5. We should ______ these ancient traditions for future generations. (preserve)
