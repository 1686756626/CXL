# Day27 测试题库｜文化+艺术（Culture & Art）

## 上午测试（9:35-9:50）｜前10词

**测试范围**：heritage, tradition, custom, ceremony, celebrate, artistic, creative, masterpiece, gallery, exhibition

### 题1（完形填空式选词）
The Great Wall is a significant part of China's cultural ______.
A. tradition  B. heritage  C. custom  D. ceremony
**答案：B**
**解析**：heritage=遗产（物质/非物质，可传承）；tradition=传统（长期做法）；custom=习俗（群体习惯）；ceremony=仪式。

### 题2（语境翻译）
It's a ______ in our family to have dinner together on Sundays.
A. heritage  B. ceremony  C. tradition  D. gallery
**答案：C**
**解析**：tradition指长期形成的家庭/社会传统做法。

### 题3（近义词辨析）
It's the local ______ to bow when greeting someone.
A. heritage  B. tradition  C. custom  D. ceremony
**答案：C**
**解析**：custom指特定地区/群体的习俗做法。

### 题4（词义理解）
The graduation ______ will be held in the university auditorium next week.
A. gallery  B. ceremony  C. exhibition  D. custom
**答案：B**
**解析**：ceremony=仪式/典礼，graduation ceremony毕业典礼。

### 题5（语境应用）
We always ______ the Spring Festival with our families.
A. exhibit  B. celebrate  C. perform  D. inspire
**答案：B**
**解析**：celebrate=庆祝（节日/事件）。

### 题6（形容词辨析）
She has a(n) ______ temperament and loves painting.
A. creative  B. artistic  C. elegant  D. classical
**答案：B**
**解析**：artistic=艺术的/有艺术天赋的（偏审美）；creative=有创造力的（偏能力）。

### 题7（词义理解）
The project encourages students to be ______ and think outside the box.
A. artistic  B. creative  C. elegant  D. classical
**答案：B**
**解析**：creative=创造性的，think outside the box（跳出框架思考）提示需要创造力。

### 题8（语境翻译）
The Mona Lisa is considered a ______ of Renaissance art.
A. gallery  B. exhibition  C. masterpiece  D. ceremony
**答案：C**
**解析**：masterpiece=杰作，大师作品。

### 题9（词义理解）
The painting is on display at the local art ______.
A. exhibition  B. gallery  C. ceremony  D. tradition
**答案：B**
**解析**：gallery=画廊/美术馆，art gallery艺术画廊。

### 题10（语境应用）
There's a new ______ of modern art at the museum this month.
A. gallery  B. ceremony  C. exhibition  D. heritage
**答案：C**
**解析**：exhibition=展览，museum exhibition博物馆展览。

## 下午测试（14:20-14:35）｜后10词

**测试范围**：performance, audience, classical, contemporary, abstract, sculpture, architecture, preserve, elegant, inspire

### 题1（词义理解）
The ______ clapped loudly after the concert ended.
A. gallery  B. audience  C. exhibition  D. ceremony
**答案：B**
**解析**：audience=观众/听众（audi=听），音乐会后的观众鼓掌。

### 题2（形容词辨析）
I prefer ______ music to pop music because it helps me relax.
A. contemporary  B. abstract  C. classical  D. creative
**答案：C**
**解析**：classical music古典音乐，与pop music流行音乐相对。

### 题3（词义理解）
The museum features ______ art from the 21st century.
A. classical  B. traditional  C. contemporary  D. elegant
**答案：C**
**解析**：contemporary=当代的/现代的（21st century提示）。

### 题4（语境翻译）
I don't understand ______ paintings—they look like random colors to me.
A. classical  B. contemporary  C. abstract  D. elegant
**答案：C**
**解析**：abstract=抽象的，random colors（随机颜色）提示抽象画。

### 题5（词义理解）
The garden is full of beautiful stone ______ from local artists.
A. exhibitions  B. sculptures  C. ceremonies  D. galleries
**答案：B**
**解析**：sculpture=雕塑，stone sculptures石雕。

### 题6（语境应用）
I really admire the ______ of ancient Chinese temples.
A. sculpture  B. architecture  C. exhibition  D. performance
**答案：B**
**解析**：architecture=建筑/建筑风格，temple architecture寺庙建筑。

### 题7（动词辨析）
We must ______ our cultural traditions for future generations.
A. celebrate  B. exhibit  C. preserve  D. inspire
**答案：C**
**解析**：preserve=保护/保存（文化/自然遗产）。

### 题8（形容词辨析）
Her dress was simple but ______, perfect for the evening party.
A. creative  B. artistic  C. elegant  D. abstract
**答案：C**
**解析**：elegant=优雅的/精美的，形容服饰得体。

### 题9（语境翻译）
The beautiful scenery ______ him to write a poem about nature.
A. preserved  B. celebrated  C. inspired  D. exhibited
**答案：C**
**解析**：inspire=激励/启发，inspire sb to do sth启发某人做某事。

### 题10（完形填空式选词）
The singer gave an amazing ______ at the concert last night.
A. exhibition  B. performance  C. ceremony  D. gallery
**答案：B**
**解析**：performance=表演/演出，concert performance音乐会表演。

## 晚间测试（20:00-20:15）｜全天20词混合

**测试范围**：全部20词 + 滚动抽查旧词（heritage, tradition, custom, ceremony, celebrate, artistic, creative, masterpiece, gallery, exhibition, performance, audience, classical, contemporary, abstract, sculpture, architecture, preserve, elegant, inspire）

### 题1（混合辨析）
The Great Wall is part of our national cultural ______, while eating dumplings during Spring Festival is a family ______.
A. heritage; tradition  B. tradition; heritage  C. custom; ceremony  D. exhibition; gallery
**答案：A**
**解析**：heritage=遗产（国家层面，可传承）；tradition=传统（家庭/社会长期做法）。

### 题2（混合辨析）
It's a local ______ to wear red on wedding days, and the wedding ______ is usually held in a hotel.
A. tradition; heritage  B. custom; ceremony  C. heritage; exhibition  D. ceremony; custom
**答案：B**
**解析**：custom=习俗（特定群体习惯）；ceremony=仪式/典礼（婚礼典礼）。

### 题3（混合辨析）
We should ______ ancient buildings because they are an important part of our cultural ______.
A. celebrate; tradition  B. preserve; heritage  C. inspire; custom  D. exhibit; ceremony
**答案：B**
**解析**：preserve=保护（建筑物）；heritage=遗产（文化层面）。

### 题4（形容词辨析）
I prefer ______ art like the Mona Lisa to ______ art that looks like random colors.
A. classical; abstract  B. abstract; classical  C. contemporary; elegant  D. artistic; creative
**答案：A**
**解析**：classical art古典艺术（如蒙娜丽莎）；abstract art抽象艺术（随机颜色）。

### 题5（语境综合）
The ______ at the concert was huge, and the singer's ______ was amazing. The whole event ______ many young people to learn music.
A. gallery; exhibition; preserved  B. audience; performance; inspired  C. ceremony; tradition; celebrated  D. custom; heritage; exhibited
**答案：B**
**解析**：audience=观众；performance=表演；inspire=激励/启发。

### 题6（旧词滚动-heritage）
The temple is part of our national ______ and must be protected for future generations.
A. tradition  B. heritage  C. custom  D. ceremony
**答案：B**

### 题7（旧词滚动-audience）
The ______ cheered when the famous pianist began to play.
A. gallery  B. exhibition  C. audience  D. ceremony
**答案：C**

### 题8（旧词滚动-preserve）
We must take action to ______ these endangered cultural traditions.
A. celebrate  B. exhibit  C. preserve  D. inspire
**答案：C**

### 题9（旧词滚动-inspire）
Her story of success ______ me to work harder at my studies.
A. preserved  B. celebrated  C. inspired  D. exhibited
**答案：C**

### 题10（旧词滚动-masterpiece）
The novel is considered a ______ of modern Chinese literature.
A. gallery  B. exhibition  C. ceremony  D. masterpiece
**答案：D**
